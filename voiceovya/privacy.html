Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

VoiceOvya records, transcribes and summarizes your meetings on your Mac. This page states the only data that leaves your machine, the data that never does, and on what basis.

What stays on your Mac

Your recordings, their transcripts, the generated summaries, your notes, your categories and your contacts are stored locally, in the application's own folder. They are never uploaded to us: no Inovya server receives them. There is no account to create, no identifier, no sync.

Local AI models (Apple Intelligence, models downloaded in the app, a runtime installed on your machine or your network) process your content without any outgoing connection.

What the application sends

Anonymous usage statistics

Processor
TelemetryDeck, servers located in the European Union
Data
Technical counters: which feature was used, how long a transcription took, which model family answered, the application and macOS versions, the interface language. An identifier derived from your device is hashed on your Mac before anything is sent.
Never
Note content, transcripts, audio, contact, participant or category names, search terms, prompt content.
Legal basis
Legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6.1.f): improving the product from anonymous usage measurements.

Crash reports

Processor
Sentry, servers located in the European Union
Data
The crash stack trace, your macOS version, your Mac model, the application version. The IP address is stripped and sending personal information is disabled. The SDK's network instrumentation is turned off, so no address of the servers you connect to is attached to a report.
Never
Anything from your notes, transcripts or audio.
Legal basis
Legitimate interest: fixing defects in the application.

Online AI models, only once you decide

If you turn "online models" on and select Claude (Anthropic) or ChatGPT (OpenAI) with your own API key, the content needed for the request is sent directly from your Mac to the provider you picked. Those exchanges pass through no Inovya server, and we have no access to them. The processing is then governed by that provider's own terms and privacy policy, under your own account; both companies are established in the United States. Until you turn that option on, nothing is sent to them.

Model downloads and local runtimes

Downloading a local model contacts Hugging Face, which hosts the files, or Apple's servers for system models. As with any download, those services see your connection's IP address; none of your data is attached to the request. If you configure a local runtime (Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp), the app connects to the address you provide, on your machine or your network, and nothing leaves it.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no advertising tracker.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No profiling, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No cookies on this website, no audience measurement tool.

Retention

Usage statistics and crash reports are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted or reduced to anonymous aggregates. Data on your Mac stays as long as you keep it: it disappears when you delete the note, or the app and its folder.

Your rights

The data the app sends is anonymous or pseudonymized and does not let us identify you: we are not able to single out "your" signals among the others (GDPR art. 11). You still hold the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability. To exercise them, or for any question: hello@voiceovya.com.

Your local data stays entirely under your control: the app can export it and delete it. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, at www.cnil.fr.
